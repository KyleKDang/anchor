## Question

Design how ratings change over time.

- Drift detection: what "position inconsistent with recorded rating" means concretely, and when it raises a flag.
- The resolution flow: the owner decides whether the rating or the ordering is stale, and what each verdict does.
- Re-rating mechanics: how a film re-enters the comparison flow and what happens to its old comparisons.
- Rewatches: what marking an already-rated film watched again does, and whether a rewatch triggers fresh comparisons.
