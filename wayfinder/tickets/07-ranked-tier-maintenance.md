## Question

Design the policy by which the engine owns the ranked tier.

- Exact size within the ~25-50 range, or whether size is dynamic.
- Re-rank cadence and triggers (new rating, profile update, time).
- Promotion from backlog and demotion of stale picks.
- Override semantics: pin, veto, force a promotion, and how overrides interact with automatic maintenance.
- Stability: the tier should evolve without churning so fast it feels random.
