## Question

Decide whether and how Anchor asks multi-criteria comparison questions beyond "which film is better": which ending hit harder, which was more tense, which score stuck, which would you rewatch tonight.

- Rule-based vs LLM-generated/interpreted questions: the open call the owner explicitly wants input on.
- Which dimensions exist and whether they are fixed or emergent.
- When such questions are asked (during the rating flow, separately, opportunistically) and how much friction is acceptable.
- How answers feed the taste profile without polluting the single total ordering, which remains the standing constraint.
- Whether multi-criteria answers create per-dimension orderings or looser signals.
